Built-in phone connector

Answer the business phone when you cannot.

Finutia connects to a business's own Vapi and Twilio accounts. Vapi handles the live call, then returns the transcript to Finutia so your assistant can help with follow-up.

What Vapi receives

Vapi receives the caller-safe business details approved for the phone assistant. It does not receive direct access to Finutia memories, connectors, or other business tools.

What happens on a call

  1. A caller reaches the Vapi-powered business number or a number forwarded to it.
  2. The assistant identifies itself as AI, uses the configured business context, and handles the live conversation.
  3. After the call, Finutia receives a normalized transcript and call details.
  4. Finutia prepares a post-call review so the owner can discuss what happened and decide what comes next.

Calls

Answer common questions and capture the caller's name, reason for calling, callback details, or appointment request.

Transcripts

Bring the completed conversation back to Finutia as text so it becomes usable business context.

Follow-up

Use connected calendars, tasks, messages, and records after the call. Vapi itself does not receive those tool permissions.

The business owner controls the Vapi and Twilio accounts and is responsible for the call notices and consent required where the business operates.

Let the call end without losing what happens next.

The transcript returns to the same assistant that helps run the rest of the office.
