Built-in connector

Bring Stripe payments into the business conversation.

Connect the Stripe account your business already owns, then ask Finutia to work with supported payment, payment-link, invoice, and customer information.

How it connects

The current connector uses a Stripe secret key supplied through Finutia's secure connector setup. The key is stored as protected credential material, not ordinary profile text.

What the connector covers

Payments

Find and understand supported payment records while discussing the customer or work they belong to.

Payment links and invoices

Prepare supported ways for customers to pay and work with Stripe invoice information.

Customers

Find supported Stripe customer records without treating Finutia as the payment system of record.

What you can ask

  • “Did this customer's payment go through?”
  • “Prepare a payment link for this amount.”
  • “Find the invoice connected to that payment.”

Stripe remains responsible for payment processing. Finutia uses the connection to help with supported work and applies your approval mode before outside actions proceed.

Keep payment questions beside the customer conversation.
