Built-in connector

Move customer documents forward with PandaDoc.

The PandaDoc connector lets Finutia create, send, and track supported documents while you stay in the conversation about the customer and the work.

How it connects

The current connector uses a PandaDoc API key supplied through Finutia's secure connector setup. PandaDoc plan and API availability still determine what the account can do.

What the connector covers

Create

Prepare a supported PandaDoc document using the information and files available for the task.

Send

Move a document to the customer when the requested action is ready and allowed.

Track

Check supported document status so the next follow-up is based on what actually happened.

What you can ask

  • “Prepare the agreement for this customer.”
  • “Send the document after I approve it.”
  • “Has the customer opened or completed the document?”

Finutia does not treat a prepared document as sent. Sending remains a separate outside action and follows your approval settings.

Keep documents tied to the customer who needs them.
