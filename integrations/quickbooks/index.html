Built-in connector

Ask Finutia about the books in QuickBooks Online.

The QuickBooks connector brings supported accounting information into the same conversation where you handle customers, schedules, and daily work.

How it connects

Finutia uses QuickBooks Online's sign-in flow. You select the company and authorize the accounting access the connector needs.

What the connector covers

Sales and customers

Work with supported customer, invoice, estimate, and payment information.

Bills and vendors

Find supported vendor, bill, and expense records without hunting through menus.

Items and reports

Use supported item information and accounting reports. Inventory quantities depend on what the connected QuickBooks plan provides.

What you can ask

  • “Which invoices are overdue?”
  • “Find the last payment from this customer.”
  • “What bills are coming due?”
  • “Show me the profit-and-loss report for this month.”

Finutia does not replace your accounting records. QuickBooks remains the connected system of record, and actions that change it follow your approval settings.
