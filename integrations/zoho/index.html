Built-in connector

Use Zoho from your Finutia conversations.

One Zoho connection can give Finutia access to the supported parts of Zoho Books, Inventory, Calendar, Mail, FSM, Payroll, and WorkDrive that your account is entitled to use.

How it connects

Finutia sends you through Zoho's sign-in and permission flow. You choose the account and approve the requested access with Zoho.

What the connector covers

Money and inventory

Work with supported customer, vendor, invoice, estimate, bill, payment, item, stock, and business-report information.

Customers and schedules

Read and work with supported Zoho Mail, Calendar, and field-service information when those services are available to the account.

Files and payroll

Reach supported WorkDrive and Payroll information through the same connected Zoho account.

What you can ask

  • “Which invoices still need attention?”
  • “Check tomorrow's calendar and tell me where I have room.”
  • “Find the customer document in WorkDrive.”
  • “Show me the inventory items running low.”

What Finutia can reach depends on the Zoho products, organization, and permissions connected to your account. Outside actions still follow your selected approval mode.

Connect only the Zoho services you use.

Finutia shows the services available through your connection instead of assuming every Zoho product is present.
