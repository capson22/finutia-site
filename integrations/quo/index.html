Built-in connector

Keep business calls, texts, and contacts connected.

The Quo connector works with the business phone service formerly known as OpenPhone, bringing supported messages, calls, and contacts into Finutia.

How it connects

The current connector uses a Quo API key supplied through Finutia's secure connector setup.

What the connector covers

Business texting

Read and send supported text messages through the connected Quo business number.

Calls

Use supported call information as part of the customer's history and the next-step conversation.

Contacts

Find and work with supported Quo contact records instead of rebuilding the customer identity by hand.

What you can ask

  • “Show me the latest messages from this customer.”
  • “Prepare a text confirming tomorrow's appointment.”
  • “Find the contact connected to this number.”

Sending a message is an outside action. Whether Finutia pauses for a press depends on the approval mode and rules you selected.

Keep customer communication beside the rest of the work.

Move from a message to the schedule, task, or record it affects.
