About Finutia

Built for the owner carrying the whole office.

Finutia AI is a business assistant built by Capson Enterprises for sole proprietors and small-business owners who need help with the work around the work.

Why Finutia exists

Running a small business often means answering calls, moving appointments, following up with customers, checking invoices, and keeping records after the workday should be over. Finutia is designed to bring that work into one conversation and help move it forward without pretending the owner is no longer responsible.

Built around the business

Your services, hours, customers, connected accounts, and working preferences give the assistant useful context.

Connected to real tools

Built-in connectors and compatible MCP servers let the assistant work with the systems the business already uses.

Visible and controlled

Approval modes and activity records keep outside actions understandable and under the owner's control.
