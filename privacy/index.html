1. Who this policy covers

Finutia AI is a business assistant operated by Capson Enterprises [confirm full legal name and “doing business as” language]. This policy describes information handled when someone visits our website, creates or uses a Finutia account, connects another service, uses an AI phone assistant, contacts support, or submits feedback.

For account, service, security, usage, and billing records, Finutia determines why and how the information is handled. For business information a customer places in Finutia, Finutia generally processes that information for the customer and under the customer's instructions. [Counsel to confirm the controller and processor language.]

2. Information Finutia handles

Account and contact information
Email address, display name, optional phone number, password hash, account status, device and session information, verification and recovery records, and acceptance of product policies.
Business and assistant content
Business profile and context, conversations, messages, summaries, memory, tasks, files, assistant preferences, automations, actions, and activity records.
Connected-service information
The account identifiers, permissions, encrypted credentials, requests, results, documents, messages, contacts, transactions, or other records needed for the services a customer chooses to connect.
Phone-assistant information
Business details selected for callers, phone settings, caller and destination identifiers, caller name and reason for calling, callback or appointment details, call timing and ending reason, transcripts, and post-call reviews. Finutia's current configuration does not record call audio or video or copy Vapi's raw call logs into Finutia.
Billing and usage information
Subscription and plan status, usage by model and provider, cost records, credit ledger, billing-event records, and Stripe customer, checkout, and payment references. Finutia does not receive full card numbers from Stripe Checkout.
Diagnostics, feedback, and support
Service and connector diagnostics, error context, feedback text, screenshots, support messages, and contact details when the person submitting feedback agrees to include them. Some diagnostics can contain message content, tool information, or third-party records.

3. How Finutia uses information

Finutia uses information to:

  • provide, personalize, secure, and troubleshoot the assistant;
  • answer questions and carry out actions under the customer's selected approval settings;
  • connect and exchange information with services the customer chooses;
  • handle AI phone calls, prepare a transcript and review, and support approved follow-up work;
  • authenticate accounts, send service emails, process subscriptions, and measure usage;
  • respond to support and feedback and protect Finutia, its customers, and other people.

The current product does not contain an advertising system and does not use customer content for targeted advertising. [Confirm whether any provider may use submitted data for model training and add the contract-specific answer before approval.]

4. AI and service providers

The providers involved depend on the model, feature, and connected accounts the customer selects. Finutia is capable of using OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and xAI for language-model requests. A request can include the business context, relevant conversation history, system instructions, tool definitions and results, supported attachments, connected-service results, or inbound customer messages needed to answer it.

Finutia's phone feature uses a customer's Twilio account for telephone service, Vapi for the live assistant, and Soniox for transcription. When warm transfer is enabled, recent call messages can be sent to OpenAI to decide whether to transfer. After a completed call, the full normalized transcript is sent to the customer's selected utility-model provider to prepare the post-call review. Vapi receives an approved caller-safe portion of the business context; it does not receive Finutia memory or direct access to other Finutia connectors and tools.

Other service-capable providers include Hetzner for hosting, GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases for this public website and its downloads, Stripe for Finutia subscriptions, Resend or SendGrid for service email, and Firecrawl for requested web search or page retrieval. An optional GitHub feedback notifier can send feedback details to a private repository when it is enabled. Public-data requests can send a vehicle identifier or description to NHTSA, a place query to OpenStreetMap Nominatim, or coordinates to the U.S. National Weather Service.

Customers can also direct Finutia to services such as Google, Zoho, QuickBooks, Quo, PandaDoc, Stripe, Vapi, Twilio, or a customer-configured MCP service. Those services receive the requests and records needed for the customer's chosen action and apply their own terms and privacy practices. [Confirm the active provider list, legal provider names, locations, data-processing terms, and subprocessor notice process before approval.]

5. Google user data

If a customer connects a Google account, Finutia accesses only the Google data covered by the permissions the customer grants and the features the customer uses. Depending on those choices, this can include profile, email, calendar, contact, or file information. Finutia uses that data to show requested information and complete customer-directed assistant work. Customers can disconnect the account in Finutia and can revoke access through their Google account.

Finutia does not sell Google user data or use it for targeted advertising. Its handling of Google user data is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. [Verify every requested Google scope and the production workflow before OAuth verification.]

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Finutia currently hosts its application, database, and stored files on a U.S. server. [Reconfirm the hosting region after the infrastructure move.] Encrypted connector credentials, content, files, transcripts, usage records, and other service records are stored for the purposes described above.

  • Detailed language-model and connector diagnostics are scheduled for deletion after seven days.
  • Files normally remain until they are deleted, unless an optional storage rule or temporary-file expiry applies.
  • Feedback remains open until it is archived and is then scheduled for permanent deletion after 90 days. Current account deletion also deletes the account's feedback. [Confirm the intended rule.]
  • Account or data deletion is scheduled after a seven-day cancellation period. Full account closure normally waits until the paid service period ends and then adds that seven-day period.
  • Deletion removes account content, files, conversations, business context, assistant data, connections, transcripts, diagnostics, and authentication records.
  • Anonymized account records, usage, subscription and billing-event records, Stripe lookup identifiers, credit-ledger entries, and deletion records can remain after account closure. [Counsel to set and approve the retention period.]

Finutia attempts to remove or revoke connected-service access during deletion, but a provider outage or a provider that does not support remote revocation can prevent that outside cleanup. Customers should also revoke Finutia directly in each provider account when closing an account.

7. Choices and controls

Depending on the feature, customers can:

  • choose the language model and connected accounts they use;
  • disconnect services and separately revoke access with the provider;
  • choose Auto, Manual, or Bypass approval settings for assistant actions;
  • configure what caller-safe business information and phone behavior Vapi uses;
  • choose whether feedback includes contact details;
  • request data-only deletion or full account closure and cancel during the grace period.

8. AI phone calls

Customers using the phone assistant are responsible for giving callers any notice and obtaining any consent required for an AI-assisted and transcribed call. Callers can volunteer sensitive information even when the assistant does not ask for it; that information can appear in the transcript and post-call review. [Counsel to approve the caller notice, consent, sensitive-data, and transcript-retention rules before public phone use.]

9. Security

Finutia uses access controls, encryption for stored connector secrets, authenticated connections, audit records, and other technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Customers are responsible for protecting their account credentials and for choosing appropriate permissions in connected services.
