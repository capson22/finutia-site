How it works

  1. Install USBridge on the device the tool is plugged into.
  2. Install USBridge on the device that needs the tool.
  3. Press Connect. The tool works as if it were plugged in there. Press Release when you are done.

Features

One device at a time
Each tool connects to one device at a time. Every device shows who has it.
Connection grade
Latency, jitter and loss are measured every second and graded A to C.
Protected Mode
For sensitive reads and writes. The device stays awake, and no one can take the device until you turn it off.
Readiness check
Checks the tool, the connection and power settings before a sensitive job.
Reconnects
A tool that drops for a moment reconnects on its own.
Job records
Each connection is recorded and can be exported for support.
Automatic updates
USBridge checks for updates once a day and installs them only when no tool is connected.
Local network or Tailscale
Devices connect over your network, or over Tailscale when they are apart.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, on each device.
  • For the Android app: Android 13 or newer.
  • Administrator rights to install.
  • On the device the tool is plugged into: usbipd-win.
  • On the device that uses the tool: usbip-win2 0.9.8.1 or newer.

The installer installs these for you.
