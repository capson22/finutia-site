After the phone stops ringing

Turn a call transcript into the next useful step.

A transcript lets the regular Finutia assistant understand what was said after Vapi finishes the live call. That is what connects phone answering to the rest of the business.

What comes back to Finutia

The phone flow returns the normalized conversation text and call details needed for a post-call review. Finutia can use that record to answer the owner's questions about the call without giving Vapi direct access to the owner's other connectors.

Understand

Ask who called, what they needed, which details they provided, and whether anything remains unresolved.

Organize

Create a task or preserve a useful note so the request does not depend on remembering the call later.

Schedule

Check a connected calendar and prepare an appointment when the caller asked for a time.

Respond

Prepare a message or another customer-facing next step using the connected service the business chose.

A transcript is business data.

Callers can volunteer sensitive information even when the assistant does not ask for it. The business owner is responsible for appropriate call notice, consent, and handling rules.

What does not happen automatically

Receiving a transcript does not give Vapi control of calendars, accounting, messages, or other Finutia connectors. Finutia evaluates later work separately, and outside actions follow the selected approval mode and tool rules.

Keep the call connected without connecting every account to the phone provider.

Vapi handles the call. Finutia handles the business conversation that follows.
