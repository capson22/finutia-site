Calls when your hands are full

How Finutia AI phone answering works.

The live call runs through Vapi using the business's own phone setup. Finutia supplies approved business context and receives the completed transcript so the call can lead to useful follow-up.

Before the first call

  1. Connect a Vapi account and the Twilio details used for the business numbers.
  2. Review the business context Finutia will use for caller-safe answers, including hours, services, and call instructions.
  3. Configure the greeting, voice, phone behavior, transfer settings, and language preferences through Atlas chat inside Finutia.
  4. Place a test call before forwarding the public business number.
Callers are told they reached an AI assistant.

The goal is a natural and helpful conversation, not hiding that the caller is speaking with AI.

During the call

Vapi handles the conversation

Vapi runs the live voice exchange using the configured assistant and phone number.

Context stays caller-safe

The phone assistant receives the approved business details it needs, not unrestricted access to Finutia memories or connected accounts.

The call stays focused

It can answer configured questions and capture the caller's name, reason, callback details, or appointment request.

After the call

Finutia receives a normalized transcript and prepares a post-call review. The owner can ask what happened, create or update a task, check a connected calendar, prepare a reply, or work with another connected account. Those later actions remain subject to the owner's approval mode.

The call is not an isolated bot session.

It becomes context your regular Finutia assistant can use for the rest of the business day.
