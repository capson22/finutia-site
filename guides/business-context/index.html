Teach the assistant the business

Accurate business context makes the assistant useful.

Business context is the information Finutia uses to understand how the company works—before it tries to answer a question, handle a caller, or prepare the next step.

What belongs in business context

Identity and services

The business name, what it does, what it sells, and the kinds of customers it serves.

Hours and coverage

Normal hours, service areas, availability, and the situations that need a human.

Working preferences

How the owner wants routine work described, prepared, approved, and followed up.

Caller-safe answers

The subset of business details approved for use by the Vapi phone assistant during a call.

Review it before enabling phone answering.

A polished voice cannot correct inaccurate hours, services, or instructions. Finutia warns the owner to verify the business context before using it with Vapi.

Business context is not the same as unrestricted access

Connecting an account does not mean every piece of its data becomes part of every prompt. Finutia selects relevant information for the work at hand. The Vapi phone assistant receives caller-safe context, not Finutia memories or direct access to the owner's other connectors and tools.

Configure it in Atlas chat

The owner can ask Atlas inside Finutia to help review and configure the business context, including phone behavior and bilingual support preferences. The owner should confirm the final details because those facts shape how the assistant represents the business.

Good context reduces repeated setup.

Enter the business once, keep it accurate, and let the relevant parts support calls and daily work.
